Kitchen Renovations Sydney Comparison Explained

When evaluating kitchen renovations sydney options, the first factor is the project type. A budget refresh that keeps the existing layout and uses standard finishes usually falls between $15,000 and $30,000. A standard full renovation that includes new cabinetry, benchtop upgrades and appliance updates typically costs $30,000 to $70,000. Premium custom rebuilds with layout changes, bespoke joinery and high‑end stone can exceed $70,000 and reach the published upper range of $100,000 plus. Timeline breakdown and key milestones

The overall programme for a Sydney kitchen renovation is roughly six to twelve weeks from sign‑off to handover, with about two to four weeks of on‑site work once cabinetry is ready. The first phase includes the free 60‑minute in‑home design consult, a one‑business‑day initial project review and the delivery of a fixed‑price quote within five to ten working days. This period is used to confirm layout decisions, plumbing or electrical point moves and any selections needed before the contract is prepared. The second phase covers demolition, cabinet installation, plumbing, electrical, benchtops, splashbacks and final fit‑off. For strata apartments, additional approvals from the body corp, lift bookings and acoustic documentation can extend the pre‑contract conversation and shift the on‑site start later.

When comparing proposals, ask for three practical dates: when the fixed price quote will arrive after the consult, when cabinetry fabrication can be scheduled once selections are confirmed, and when site work is expected to begin assuming approvals and access are in place. This structure makes it easier to see whether two offers that seem similar rely on different assumptions about the approval stage and the on‑site window.

Heritage and strata considerations

Older houses such as heritage terraces, post‑war brick homes and Federation style properties require careful joinery, service upgrades and finishes that respect the age of the house. The target page advises homeowners to ask what needs to be confirmed during measurement before cabinetry manufacture starts, ensuring that any structural work or heritage constraints are identified early. For strata apartments, the builder must handle body corp documentation, plumbing through party walls, acoustic underlay and after‑hours work windows. The money_home_text highlights that renovators experienced with Newtown, Surry Hills, Mosman, Randwick and Ryde heritage briefs, as well as Bondi Junction, Randwick and Alexandria strata committees, are better suited to these projects.

These conditions affect the pre‑contract conversation more than the style brief. In an older home, clarify any required heritage approvals before ordering cabinets. In an apartment, confirm who manages committee paperwork, what building rules apply to waste removal and whether lift bookings or restricted work hours could affect the programme. These details shape when site work can actually begin and influence the overall timeline.
